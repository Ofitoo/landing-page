2. Garmin Data Management

Ofitoo does not use your Garmin data for personal purposes. All fitness and health data synchronized from Garmin is used exclusively to provide you with insights, calculations, and tracking within the Ofitoo application.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: name, email address, date of birth, gender
  • Biometric Data: weight, height, body measurements
  • Health & Fitness Data: calorie intake, physical activity, workout data, nutrition information
  • Device Data: fitness tracker data, including heart rate, steps, GPS location, speed, altitude, and other metrics from connected devices
  • Usage Data: how you interact with our app and services

4. Third-Party Integrations

Garmin Connect Integration:

When you connect your Garmin device or Garmin Connect account to Ofitoo, we collect and process the following data:

  • Activity data (steps, distance, calories burned)
  • Heart rate and other biometric measurements
  • GPS location data during workouts
  • Sleep data and stress levels
  • Workout summaries and exercise details

This data is used solely to:

  • Display your fitness metrics within the Ofitoo app
  • Calculate your daily calorie balance
  • Provide personalized fitness recommendations
  • Track your progress toward health goals

Ofitoo uses your Garmin data exclusively for the purpose of providing its core fitness services. No personal use of this data is made.
